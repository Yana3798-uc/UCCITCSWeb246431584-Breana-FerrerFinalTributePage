Walt Disney

A dreamer who turned imagination into worlds

Walt Disney was an American film producer and entrepreneur who co-founded what became one of the world’s most influential entertainment studios and helped pioneer animated feature films.

He created iconic characters like Mickey Mouse, oversaw classics such as Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, and envisioned theme parks like Disneyland and Walt Disney World that continue to delight visitors worldwide.

  • Born December 5, 1901, in Chicago, Illinois.
  • Won dozens of Academy Awards across his career.
  • His legacy lives on through films, television, and global theme parks.
